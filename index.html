Questions

Quick answers before you message us.

How do I order?

Tap Order under the item you like. WhatsApp opens with a message ready to send. We reply with photos and the final price, then you pay and collect in store or ask us to deliver in town.

Can I see the item before I pay?

Yes. We send photos and videos on WhatsApp, and you can visit our shop at Cyrus Mall to look and try on.

How can I pay?

You can pay with cash or EcoCash.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver within Harare town (CBD). Message us on WhatsApp with your location and we will arrange it.

Where are you and when are you open?

We are at Cyrus Mall, shop C17, on the corner of Mbuya Nehanda and Speke, opposite Chicken Slice. Opening hours are listed under Visit us below.
